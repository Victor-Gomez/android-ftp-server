Privacy Policy

App: Android FTP Server
Developer: Victor Gomez (victorgomez.studio)
Effective Date: September 14, 2026
Summary: Android FTP Server does not collect, track, store, or share any personal information, files, or telemetry. All transfers take place strictly point-to-point over your local Wi-Fi network.

1. Overview

Android FTP Server is a local network utility designed to let you transfer files between your Android device and PC/Mac without cables or external cloud services. The app runs directly on your device and only transmits data within your local Wi-Fi network.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account details are collected.
  • No File Uploads to Cloud: Your files never leave your device unless you explicitly transfer them to a computer connected to your local network.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The application contains no tracking SDKs, ad networks, or analytics frameworks.

3. Permissions Used by the App

  • Storage Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required to allow reading and writing files via FTP or the Web file manager as requested by authorized client connections on your local Wi-Fi network.
  • Wi-Fi & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE): Required to bind the FTP and HTTP server sockets to your local device IP address.
  • Foreground Service: Ensures file transfers are not terminated when your screen turns off or when you switch apps.
  • Notifications: Displays active server status, current connection URLs, and quick-stop controls.
